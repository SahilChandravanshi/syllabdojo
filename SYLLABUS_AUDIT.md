# SyllabDojo syllabus audit — 28 October 2026

## What this pass changes

- Expanded the topic checklist for Reasoning, English, Computer Knowledge, General Awareness, banking/financial awareness, RBI ESI/Finance/Management, SEBI General Stream papers, and NABARD ESI/ARD.
- Kept existing topic IDs stable wherever a topic already existed, so saved completion state remains compatible. Newly added topics have new IDs and start unchecked.
- Removed outdated exact SBI PO pattern figures and replaced them with the current 2026 notification structure: Prelims 40/30/30 questions with 20 minutes per section; Mains 170 objective questions for 200 marks over 3 hours plus a 30-mark descriptive paper over 30 minutes. The tracker groups Reasoning and Computer Aptitude together, and General/Economy/Banking Knowledge together, so combined section totals are not meant to be added multiple times.
- Corrected SEBI Grade A General Stream pattern to Phase I 200 marks over 100 minutes and Phase II 200 marks over 150 minutes (English descriptive Paper I: 100 marks/60 minutes; General Stream Paper II: 100 marks/90 minutes).
- Updated IBPS PO pattern display to avoid relying on an unverified legacy section-by-section breakdown; users are directed to the current recruitment-cycle notification for exact splits.
- Updated the data version string to `2026.10.28-syllabus-audit-v7`.

## Important scope note

IBPS, SBI, RRB, RBI, SEBI, NABARD and SSC notifications do not all publish the same level of topic-by-topic detail. For several competitive exams, the fine-grained topics are a practical preparation checklist derived from the official subject/paper structure and established exam-preparation coverage; they should not be represented as a verbatim official exhaustive syllabus. Recruitment-cycle pattern details can change and should be checked against the latest notice.

## Remaining review caveat

The current app tracks General Stream for SEBI and General/RDBS for NABARD. Specialist streams are intentionally outside the supported exam scope. A topic checklist can be comprehensive for preparation without being a guarantee that every possible question topic has been enumerated.
