# SyllabDojo v37 — Heatmap ranges and topic details

- Added Last 12 months, Year, Last month, and Last week heatmap range filters.
- Heatmap date pills are clickable and open the date's recorded topic-completion details, grouped by subject.
- New topic completions are recorded by exam and date and included in local progress export/import.
- Historical dates only have counts from older versions; topic-level detail is available from the date the new tracking is installed onward.
- Existing exam selection sync and monochrome styling are preserved.
